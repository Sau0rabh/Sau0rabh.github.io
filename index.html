About Me

Bridging electrical theory with real-world plant systems.

I am a final-year Electrical Engineering undergraduate at Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology (MMMUT), Gorakhpur. My academic journey is driven by a deep fascination with how electrical power is generated, distributed, protected, and intelligently automated across modern industrial infrastructure.

During my project engineering internship at Maruti Suzuki India Limited (Gurugram), I worked inside the high-precision Paint Shop Maintenance Department. I gained first-hand industrial exposure to Mitsubishi MELSEC PLCs, GX Works2 Ladder Logic programming, sensor-actuator interlocks, VFD drives, and diagnosed root causes across 15+ breakdown cases to keep assembly lines running smoothly.

Earlier, during my training at UPPTCL, I studied the operational architectures of 400 kV extra-high-voltage substations, power transformers, SF6 switchgear, and numerical protection relay coordination. Complementing power systems, I build embedded IoT control systems using ESP32 and Arduino for precision automated agriculture.
